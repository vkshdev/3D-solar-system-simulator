Milky Way Galaxy Simulator

An interactive, scientific real-time 3D space simulation built with WebGL, Three.js, and Rust WebAssembly physics.

Solar System Exploration

Simulate accurate orbital mechanics and scale models of the Sun, Mercury, Venus, Earth and Moon, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn with ring systems, Uranus, Neptune, and realistic comets.

Milky Way Galaxy & Supermassive Black Hole

Journey through 100,000 light-years of our galaxy featuring 150,000 dynamically rendered stars, logarithmic spiral arms (Perseus, Scutum-Centaurus, Sagittarius, Outer), interstellar dust lanes, bipolar Fermi gamma-ray bubbles, and Sagittarius A* relativistic Kerr black hole accretion disk with gravitational lensing.
